Privacy Policy

PropFirm Edge · Last updated: October 12, 2026

This policy describes how PropFirm Edge handles information in the Android and other app versions. PropFirm Edge is a simulation, challenge-planning, trading-journal, and performance-tracking tool. It does not connect to a broker, execute trades, hold funds, or require an account.

Information stored on your device

The app stores information you enter or import locally so its features work. This can include challenge settings and progress, account or firm names, balances and equity values, journal entries and trade details, imported CSV-derived records, and simulation or optimization history. CSV files are selected and parsed on your device; the app does not upload the original file.

The app uses Android's application storage. It does not add its own encryption layer to these local records. You can remove them by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. Do not enter information you do not want stored on this device.

Information sent to the calculation service

When you choose to run a free simulation, the app sends the challenge parameters and simulation inputs needed to calculate the result. When you choose optimization using journal or CSV data, the app sends the challenge parameters and the parsed trade-return values needed for that calculation; it does not send the source CSV file. These requests are sent over HTTPS to the PropFirm Edge backend hosted by Render.

The backend uses the request values in memory to calculate and return a response. It does not save request bodies or calculation results in an application database. Request contents are not written to the service access logs.

Network and service logs

The backend and its hosting provider may process your IP address, request path, response status, and timing information for rate limiting, service operation, and troubleshooting. The rate limiter keeps an IP address in process memory for up to 60 seconds. Render service logs may contain the IP address, request path, and status. Under the current Render Hobby plan, Render retains logs for 7 days; retention can change if the hosting plan changes. Render provides hosting and log processing as a service provider.

We do not use this information for advertising, sell it, or use IP addresses to infer your location. We do not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs in the app.

Security

App-to-backend requests use HTTPS, which encrypts information in transit. Local records are kept in the app's device storage and are not separately encrypted by the app.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain on your device until you delete them by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app. Simulation inputs and results are used in memory for the request and are not retained in an application database. Service access logs are subject to the log retention period described above and are deleted by the hosting provider when that period expires.

Children

PropFirm Edge is not designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. Questions about this policy can be sent to clapps.ai@gmail.com.

This app provides informational simulations and record-keeping tools. Results are scenarios, not predictions or financial advice.
